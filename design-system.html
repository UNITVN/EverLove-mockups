Tab bar & Home tiles

Home · Pet · Camera · Chat · Us. Frosted capsule, filled glyphs, no labels. The active tab gets a wide pill and an orchid-deep glyph; inactive glyphs are plum-deep. The Home glyph carries swap arrows: tapping the active Home tab opens the homepage view switcher (Status, Map, Pet).

Home tile row: pastel squircles with illustrated icons. Locked tiles (no partner yet) are desaturated with a lock badge.

Rules

Body 17px, section labels 13px, tab bar has no visible labels (aria-label only). Touch targets at least 44px. Glass only on sticky nav and tab layers. Selected state = orchid-deep outline plus check. Premium = gold crown on a plum disc. Status bar pinned; first child of .device-content owns the 59px safe area.

Deviation: the reference is plum and orchid, which is purple-family. The skill's iOS no-purple rule was overridden at the user's request.
